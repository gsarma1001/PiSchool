## What is The Pi School?

The Pi School offers personalized, high-quality math tutoring for students
of all levels from foundational skills to advanced classes like AP
Calculus. We offer one-on-one, semi-private, and group sessions both in
person and online.

## What types of classes are offered?

Private Tutoring - One-on-one support tailored to your needs. Semi-Private
Tutoring - 2 students sessions for peers at similar levels. Group Tutoring
- Larger sessions (3+ students) ideal for similar-level collaborative
learning.

## Where are classes held?

Most classes are held online, but some in person accommodations can be
worked out in Bothell and Redmond.

## How long are the sessions and how often do they occur?

Each session is 1 hour long and typically held once a week. Frequency can
be adjusted based on need.

## Can I join with friends for group sessions?

Yes! If your friends are at similar math levels, group lessons are a great
way to learn together. If not, consider semi-private or individual
tutoring.

## What happens during a typical class?

Classes include a review of school topics, reinforcement of key math
concepts, and problem-solving based on student needs. Homework help is
always welcome.

## What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled session are not
eligible for refunds or make-up classes.
